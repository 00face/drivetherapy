Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Version: 2.2.0

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Compliance):
Drive Therapy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. What Data Drive Therapy Accesses

  • Google Account Profile (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): Used strictly to display your active signed-in account name, avatar, and email in the studio header and multi-account switcher.
  • Google Drive Metadata & Media Streams (drive.file, drive.readonly): Used to list folders you browse, stream image/audio/video previews directly to your browser, and save user-initiated non-destructive sidecars (such as .xmp rating files) when explicitly confirmed by you.
  • Google Photos Picker Items (photospicker.mediaitems.readonly): Used only when you explicitly launch the Google Photos Picker to import selected photos into your local curation catalog.

2. How Data Is Stored & Protected

  • Local-First Storage: Your curation ratings, picks, rejects, tags, and LUT adjustments are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB (drive_therapy_catalog_v2).
  • Encrypted Server Session Vault: OAuth 2.0 tokens are stored in an AES-256-GCM encrypted session vault (.session-vault.enc) on your Drive Therapy host and referenced via an HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax cookie (dt_sid). Raw OAuth tokens are never stored in browser localStorage.
  • Zero Sale or AI Training on User Data: Drive Therapy never sells, rents, transfers to advertising brokers, or trains foundational AI models on your Google Drive or Google Workspace data.

3. Data Revocation & Zero-Residue Deletion

You can sign out of any connected Google account or trigger a complete Factory Reset inside Storage Settings → Factory Reset (POST /api/system/factory-reset), which immediately revokes and deletes the encrypted session vault, clears all IndexedDB databases, and sends Clear-Site-Data: "cache", "cookies", "storage".
